# Retail-Sales-Performance-Analysis-Dashboard
Excel-based Retail Sales Performance Dashboard with KPI tracking, trend analysis, and interactive slicers.
## Project Objective
To build an interactive Excel dashboard that transforms retail sales data into meaningful business insights by tracking KPIs, sales trends, customer performance, regional sales, product profitability, and other key metrics.
## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the Total Sales generated?
- What is the Net Sales after discounts?
- How many Total Orders were placed?
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
- What is the Average Discount offered?
- What is the Average Customer Rating?
- How do Monthly Sales vary throughout the year?
- Which Region generates the highest sales?
- Which Ship Mode contributes the most to sales?
- Which Product Category contributes the highest share of sales?
- Which Customer Segment generates the most revenue?
- Who are the Top 10 Customers by sales?
- Which are the Top 5 Most Profitable Products?
- Which are the Bottom 5 Least Profitable Products?
- Which States generate the highest profit?
## Process
- Collected and reviewed the retail sales dataset.
- Cleaned the data by removing duplicates, handling missing values, and correcting data formats.
- Validated the dataset to ensure data accuracy and consistency.
- Created calculated fields and KPIs such as Total Sales, Net Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, Average Order Value (AOV), Average Discount, and Average Rating.
- Analyzed sales performance across months, regions, ship modes, product categories, customer segments, customers, products, and states.
- Built Pivot Tables to summarize the data for analysis.
- Designed interactive charts and visualizations to present key business insights.
- Added slicers for Region, Category, Segment, Ship Mode, and Rating to enable dynamic filtering.
- Developed a clean, user-friendly dashboard layout with consistent formatting and color themes.
## Project Insight
- The business generated 1,975,258 in total sales from 9,994 customer orders.
- Net Sales reached 1,946,377.75, indicating that discounts had a relatively small impact on overall revenue.
- The Average Order Value (AOV) was 3,885.74, showing a healthy average spend per order.
- West and East regions contributed the highest sales, while the South region had the lowest sales performance.
- Standard Class was the most preferred shipping mode, contributing the largest share of total sales.
- The Consumer segment generated the highest revenue, making it the most valuable customer segment.
- Technology was the highest-selling product category, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.
- The monthly sales trend showed fluctuations throughout the year, with stronger sales during the final months, indicating seasonal demand.
- A small group of customers contributed a significant portion of total sales, highlighting the importance of retaining high-value customers.
- Profitability varied across products, with a few products generating substantial profits while others underperformed, emphasizing the need to optimize the product portfolio.
- Profit distribution across states revealed that some states consistently outperformed others, providing opportunities for region-specific business strategies.
- Interactive slicers enabled dynamic analysis by Region, Category, Segment, Ship Mode, and Rating, allowing users to explore the data from multiple business perspectives.
## Final Conclusion:
The Retail Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance by transforming raw sales data into actionable insights. Through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking, the dashboard enables users to monitor sales trends, evaluate customer behavior, analyze regional performance, and identify profitable products and customer segments.

The analysis highlights that Technology is the leading product category, the Consumer segment contributes the highest revenue, and Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode. Regional and profitability analyses help identify high-performing areas as well as opportunities for improvement. Interactive slicers allow users to explore the data dynamically and make informed, data-driven decisions.

Overall, this project demonstrates the effective use of Microsoft Excel for data cleaning, analysis, KPI reporting, and dashboard development, showcasing practical business intelligence skills and supporting strategic decision-making through clear and interactive reporting.
